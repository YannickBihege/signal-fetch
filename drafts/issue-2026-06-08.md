## Framework

The Signal measures every position not in nominal currency terms but in ounces of gold. This approach strips out the noise of monetary inflation and currency fluctuation, expressing each holding's true purchasing-power value against a hard-money benchmark. A position that rises in euros but falls in gold terms is, by this measure, losing real value.

Entry and exit discipline is guided by the 14-period Relative Strength Index (RSI-14):

| RSI-14 | Zone | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| Below 40 | Oversold | BUY |
| 40 – 70 | Neutral | HOLD |
| Above 70 | Overbought | NO ENTRY |

No position is added to while it sits in the NO ENTRY zone, and no forced selling occurs on HOLD. The framework is patient by design.
