## Framework

The Signal measures every position not in nominal currency terms but in ounces of gold — a supply-constrained, politically neutral store of value. Denominating equity holdings in gold strips out the noise of monetary expansion and currency fluctuation, revealing whether a position is genuinely appreciating in real purchasing-power terms or merely riding a depreciating unit of account.

Position sizing, entry, and exit decisions are guided by the RSI-14 momentum indicator applied to each stock's USD price. The rules are simple and applied consistently:

| RSI-14 Reading | Signal |
|---|---|
| Below 40 | **BUY** — oversold; consider initiating or adding |
| 40 to 70 | **HOLD** — momentum neutral; no new capital deployed |
| Above 70 | **NO ENTRY** — overbought; avoid adding, review exposure |

ASML, the portfolio's largest gold-equivalent holding at 0.4289 oz, absorbed a -2.55% drawdown in fiat terms; in gold terms, that premium has similarly eroded, reinforcing the importance of patient, threshold-based accumulation rather than reactive trading.

The standout event this week is the sharp single-session decline in OKLO, which shed over 8.5% and has now crossed into technically oversold territory with an RSI-14 of 31.6. Any specific catalyst for OKLO's drop should be assessed before acting; however, from a purely mechanical standpoint, the RSI signal is clear and the gold-equivalent cost basis — just 0.0098 oz — reflects a position that remains extremely small relative to the rest of the book.
