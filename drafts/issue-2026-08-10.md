## Framework

This newsletter evaluates positions not in nominal currency terms, but relative to gold — the portfolio's benchmark and unit of account. Each holding's price is expressed in troy ounces of gold, allowing for inflation-adjusted, currency-neutral performance tracking. A position gaining in USD but losing ground in gold terms is, by this framework, a deteriorating position.

Entry and exit discipline is guided by the 14-period Relative Strength Index (RSI-14):

| RSI-14 Range | Signal |
|---|---|
| RSI < 40 | **BUY** — oversold, look to add |
| RSI 40–70 | **HOLD** — momentum neutral, maintain position |
| RSI > 70 | **NO ENTRY** — overbought, avoid initiating or adding |

## This week

Against this backdrop, the semiconductor complex broadly held its ground this week, though the divergence in gold-denominated performance remains the more instructive metric. The standout event of the week is OKLO's near 15% single-session move, which warrants close attention. The advanced nuclear company has been a beneficiary of surging AI data centre power demand narratives, and any positive regulatory or partnership development in that context could explain the spike. However, at just 0.0111 oz gold, OKLO remains the smallest position in the portfolio by a considerable margin, and its RSI of 55.4, while elevated from where it likely began the week, has not yet crossed into overbought territory. Momentum is constructive but disciplined sizing remains appropriate.

AMD is the lone decliner this week at -1.21%, and its RSI of 46.9 places it closest to the lower boundary of the HOLD zone. This is worth monitoring — any further deterioration in price that drags RSI below 40 would trigger a formal BUY signal and a potential opportunity to add at improved gold-denominated terms. The broader macro context, including ongoing AI capital expenditure cycles and any Federal Reserve communication this week, will likely drive near-term volatility across all five positions.
