## Framework

The Signal measures every holding not in nominal currency terms but in ounces of gold — a supply-constrained, politically neutral store of value. By denominating positions in gold, we strip away the noise of dollar or euro fluctuation and ask a simpler question: is this company gaining or losing purchasing power in real terms?

Entry and exit timing is guided by the 14-period Relative Strength Index (RSI-14), applied to each position's price in USD. The rules are as follows:

| RSI-14 Range | Signal |
|---|---|
| RSI < 40 | **BUY** — asset is oversold; consider initiating or adding |
| RSI 40–70 | **HOLD** — momentum is neutral; maintain existing positions |
| RSI > 70 | **NO ENTRY** — asset is overbought; avoid new capital deployment |

That context underscores the importance of selectivity: the four HOLD positions are broadly preserving their gold-denominated values, but none is making decisive upside progress against the benchmark this week.

The notable development is OKLO's sharp single-session decline of −9.18%, bringing its RSI-14 below the 40 threshold and triggering a formal BUY signal.
