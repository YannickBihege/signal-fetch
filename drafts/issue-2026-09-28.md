## Framework

The Signal measures every position not in nominal currency terms, but in ounces of gold. This approach strips away the distortions of monetary inflation and currency fluctuation, grounding portfolio performance in a hard, supply-constrained benchmark. A stock that rises in dollar terms but falls in gold terms is, by this measure, losing ground in real wealth. Only positions that gain in gold oz over time are considered to be compounding genuine value.

Entry and exit discipline is guided by the 14-period Relative Strength Index (RSI-14), applied as follows:

| RSI-14 Range | Signal |
|---|---|
| Below 40 | **BUY** — position is oversold; accumulation favoured |
| 40 – 70 | **HOLD** — momentum is neutral to constructive; no new entry |
| Above 70 | **NO ENTRY** — position is overbought; avoid adding exposure |

Existing holders in a NO ENTRY zone are not required to sell; the signal simply cautions against adding new capital at stretched levels.
