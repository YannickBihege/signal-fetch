## Framework

The Signal measures every position not in nominal currency terms but in ounces of gold. This approach strips away the distortions of monetary inflation and exchange-rate drift, anchoring valuation in a historically stable unit of account. A stock that rises in euros but falls in gold terms is, by this measure, losing real value. Accumulation and trimming decisions are therefore driven by the gold-denominated price trend, not the fiat headline.

Timing signals are provided by the 14-period Relative Strength Index (RSI-14), applied to the gold-denominated price series. The rules are simple and consistent:

| RSI-14 | Signal | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| < 40 | **BUY** | Oversold relative to gold; favourable entry |
| 40 – 70 | **HOLD** | Neutral momentum; no action required |
| > 70 | **NO ENTRY** | Overbought relative to gold; avoid adding |
