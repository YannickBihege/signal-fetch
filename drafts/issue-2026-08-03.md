## Framework

The Signal measures every position not in nominal currency terms, but in ounces of gold. By anchoring valuations to gold spot, we strip out the noise of monetary inflation and currency fluctuation, asking the only question that matters: is this company gaining or losing purchasing power relative to the hardest benchmark available?

RSI-14 readings guide entry discipline. The rules are simple and applied without exception:

| RSI-14 | Signal |
|---|---|
| < 40 | BUY — oversold, consider initiating or adding |
| 40 – 70 | HOLD — momentum neutral, no new capital deployed |
| > 70 | NO ENTRY — overbought, avoid chasing |

Positions are reviewed weekly. All gold valuations use the Monday snapshot rate.
