## Framework

The Signal measures every position not in nominal currency terms, but in ounces of gold. This approach strips out the distortions of monetary inflation and currency fluctuation, anchoring valuations to a supply-constrained, politically neutral store of value. A stock must appreciate in gold terms — not merely in euros or dollars — to represent genuine wealth creation.

Entry and exit discipline is guided by the 14-period Relative Strength Index (RSI-14), applied as follows:

| RSI-14 Range | Signal |
|---|---|
| Below 40 | **BUY** — position is oversold relative to trend |
| 40 – 70 | **HOLD** — momentum is neutral to constructive |
| Above 70 | **NO ENTRY** — position is overbought; await pullback |

No action is taken solely on price movement in fiat terms. The gold-denominated oz value and RSI gate every decision.
