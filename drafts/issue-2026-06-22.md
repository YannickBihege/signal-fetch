## Framework

The Signal measures every position not only in euros but in troy ounces of gold. Pricing equities against a monetary metal strips out the noise of currency debasement and central-bank policy, leaving a cleaner read on whether a stock is genuinely appreciating in real terms or simply riding a weaker fiat tide. A position that rises in euros but falls in gold ounces is, by this framework, losing ground.

Entry and exit discipline is guided by the 14-period RSI on each position's USD price. The rules are simple and applied consistently:

| RSI-14 | Signal |
|---|---|
| < 40 | **BUY** — oversold, seek entry |
| 40 – 70 | **HOLD** — momentum is constructive but not extended |
| > 70 | **NO ENTRY** — overbought, wait for reset |
