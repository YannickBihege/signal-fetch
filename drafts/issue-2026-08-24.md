## Framework

The Signal measures every position not in nominal currency terms but in ounces of gold. This approach strips out the distortions of monetary inflation and currency fluctuation, anchoring portfolio performance to a stable, historically durable store of value. A stock that rises in dollar terms but falls in gold terms is, by this measure, losing real purchasing power. Accumulation and trimming decisions are therefore guided by gold-denominated value, not nominal price.

Entry and exit signals are filtered through the 14-period Relative Strength Index (RSI-14) to avoid chasing momentum extremes:

| RSI-14 Range | Signal |
|---|---|
| Below 40 | **BUY** — Oversold; favourable accumulation zone |
| 40 – 70 | **HOLD** — Neutral; no new capital deployed |
| Above 70 | **NO ENTRY** — Overbought; avoid initiating or adding |
