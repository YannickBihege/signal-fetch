## Framework

The Signal measures every holding not in nominal currency terms, but in ounces of gold. This approach strips out the noise of monetary inflation and currency fluctuation, forcing a cleaner question: is this company genuinely creating value, or simply riding a depreciating unit of account? Gold spot is used as the denominator at each weekly snapshot, with EUR prices as the primary reporting currency (EUR/USD: 1.1567 this week; gold: €3,812.44/oz).

Position sizing, entry discipline, and exit decisions are guided in part by the 14-period Relative Strength Index (RSI-14), applied as follows:

| RSI-14 Range | Signal |
|---|---|
| Below 40 | **BUY** — asset is oversold relative to recent momentum |
| 40 – 70 | **HOLD** — momentum is neutral; maintain existing positions |
| Above 70 | **NO ENTRY** — asset is overbought; avoid initiating new positions |

RSI is a supporting indicator, not a standalone trigger. Gold-denominated value trends take precedence in position assessment.

The EUR/USD rate at 1.1567 reflects a dollar that has softened meaningfully over the past year, which flatters USD-denominated price gains when converted to euros but does not change the gold-denominated reality: these positions must appreciate in absolute terms to hold their real value against a rising gold floor.
